## The entry

> He wrote the law that will govern every algorithm in the country, then printed the bill, photographed the printout with his phone, and asked an intern to turn the photo back into a PDF.

## Why it fits the judge criteria

- **One sentence, on the subject:** a single satirical sentence roasting a lawmaker who regulates AI but cannot convert a file to PDF.
- **A real turn:** it opens on the lawmaker's authority — he wrote the law that will govern every algorithm in the country — then undercuts it with the file he cannot convert.
- **Aim on power and competence:** the joke is about the lawmaker and the rules he writes, not random abuse.
- **Clean in one pass, punch at the end:** the line reads straight through and the punch lands on the last few words — turning the photo *back* into a PDF.
- **A specific, unexpected detail:** not a generic "he can't use a computer" gag, but a concrete scene — printing the bill, photographing the printout with his phone, and handing it to an intern.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` creates `/output` if needed, clears only the contents of `/output` (never the directory itself), then writes the single sentence as UTF-8 to `/output/entry.txt`. It uses no network and no input files.
